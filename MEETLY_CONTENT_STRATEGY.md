## 1. Editorial Vision & Content Philosophy

Meetly's content strategy is designed to capture **top-of-funnel (TOFU) and middle-of-funnel (MOFU) search queries** from adults seeking genuine social connection, friendship advice, and casual conversation.

### Core Editorial Principles
1. **Empathy Without Medical Claims:** We acknowledge loneliness and boredom as universal human experiences, but we **never** position Meetly as therapy, psychiatric care, or a crisis intervention tool. All emotional-wellbeing topics include verified helpline references.
2. **Actionable & Practical:** Articles avoid vague philosophical fluff. Every piece provides tangible scripts, conversation starters, psychological insights, and safety checklists.
3. **Natural Product Integration:** Rather than aggressive popups or intrusive banners, Meetly's features (interest tags, ephemeral text, mutual opt-in video, skip/block controls) are introduced naturally as practical solutions to the problems discussed.
4. **Safety & Legal Integrity:** All content emphasizes adult-only participation (18+), boundary setting, and zero tolerance for harassment in compliance with India's IT Intermediary Rules (Rule 3(2)) and the DPDP Act 2023.
